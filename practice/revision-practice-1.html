SAT Math Practice Test 1

54 original CLC practice questions across two full Math modules, covering Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem-Solving & Data Analysis, and Geometry & Trigonometry.

Built on CLC's long experience in Digital SAT preparation. This is an original 54-question Digital SAT Math revision set, developed by Tsalapatis Stavros, combining two full Module-style sections mixing all four Math domains, each with a full CLC review: Formula, Tip, Trap, Fastest Way, and Desmos steps.
  • 54 questions across two full modules, mixing Algebra, Advanced Math, Problem-Solving & Data Analysis, and Geometry & Trigonometry.
  • Multiple-choice questions: choose one answer. Student-produced response questions: type your answer (fractions like 7/2 or decimals are accepted).
  • Use Flag to mark questions to return to. The Desmos calculator is available at the top at all times.
  • When time runs out, the test is submitted automatically.
